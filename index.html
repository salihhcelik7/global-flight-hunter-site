About Global Flight Hunter

Global Flight Hunter is an AI-assisted global flight deal discovery and route optimisation application, currently in active development.

Instead of requiring a fixed origin, destination and date, it starts from a budget and a time window and searches for viable destinations worldwide. It builds alternative itineraries — nearby departure airports, flexible dates, split tickets and self-transfer combinations — that conventional search does not surface, and scores every fare against that route's own price history so travellers can tell a genuine deal from an ordinary one.

The product is not yet released and does not sell tickets. This page is the project's public information page.
