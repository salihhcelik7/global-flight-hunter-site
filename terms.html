2. What the app is — and what it is not

The app is a flight price discovery and price-intelligence tool. It shows prices it has observed, compares a route's current price against that route's own observed history, and points out when a price looks unusual.

The app is not a travel agency, an airline, a booking engine, a payment processor or an intermediary in a purchase. Specifically:

  • We do not sell tickets. There is no purchase flow in the app.
  • We take no payment. The app has no payment screen, no card entry, no in-app purchase and no subscription.
  • We do not make, hold, change or cancel reservations. We have no ability to do any of those things.
  • We are not a party to any contract you enter into with an airline or a booking site.

LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED: Confirm that no Turkish travel-agency licensing requirement (TÜRSAB / 1618 sayılı Kanun) is triggered by displaying prices and linking onward without taking payment. This is the single most important regulatory question about the product and must be answered before launch.

3. Prices are observed, not quoted

This is the most important thing in this document.

Every price in the app is an observation. It is a record of what a named data source reported at a stated moment in the past. Each price is shown together with:

  • where it came from — the data source is labelled on the card, and the full list of sources is shown in the app's settings;
  • when it was seen — an observation timestamp and a freshness state (live / cached hours / cached days / estimated).

An observed price is not an offer, a quotation, a guarantee, or a promise that a ticket at that price exists now. Airline pricing changes continuously; a fare visible in the app may have been sold, withdrawn or repriced before you reach the booking site. Prices may also be shown in a currency converted from the source's own currency.

Where the app cannot support a claim with data, it says so instead of guessing: a route with too few observations is shown without a deal score rather than with an invented one, and an empty area on the map means "we have seen nothing here", not "there is nothing here".

LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED: Confirm this section is sufficient to prevent a displayed price from being construed as an offer or an invitation to treat under Turkish contract law, and that Turkish consumer-protection rules on price advertising (TKHK / fiyat etiketi ve reklam mevzuatı) are satisfied by the source-and-timestamp labelling described here.

Affiliate disclosure. Some onward links may carry an affiliate identifier, which means we could earn a commission if you book after following one. This never changes the price you pay and never changes the ranking of results: the app ranks by its own deal score, computed from observed price history, with no commission input.

Note on current state: the app does not yet open any booking link. The link is produced by our backend and carried in the API response, but no screen opens it. This clause is written for the moment that flow is connected, and must be confirmed as accurate at that time.

5. Separate tickets and self-transfer

The app deliberately surfaces itineraries assembled from separate tickets, because that is often where the saving is. It labels them every time.

If you buy separate tickets:

  • the transfer is your responsibility. No airline is protecting the connection.
  • a delay on the first ticket gives you no rights on the second. You may have to buy a new ticket at your own cost.
  • baggage is usually not through-checked. You may need to collect, clear customs and re-check it, which takes time the app estimates but cannot guarantee.
  • you may need a visa or a transit permit for a country you only intended to pass through.

The app shows connection risk (low / medium / high), the time available, the minimum connection time it believes is required, and whether the transfer involves a terminal or airport change. These are estimates. They are computed from schedule data and general rules, not from any airline's own guarantee.

You decide whether to accept that risk. We are not liable for a missed connection, a lost fare, an unusable ticket, an immigration refusal, or any other consequence of choosing a separate-ticket itinerary.

LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED: Confirm whether a liability disclaimer of this kind is enforceable against a consumer in Turkey, and whether presenting self-transfer options at all creates any duty of care that a disclaimer cannot remove. Consider whether an explicit in-app acknowledgement should be required before a separate-ticket itinerary can be opened.

6. "Deal", "cheap" and what those labels mean

When the app calls a price a deal, it means one thing and only one thing: the price is unusual compared with what we have observed for that same route before. The comparison is against our own observation history for that route and trip shape, over a stated window, with a stated sample size.

The app never claims, and you should never read it as claiming:

  • that this is the cheapest price available anywhere;
  • that no cheaper option exists;
  • that every airline or every seller has been checked;
  • that the price will still be there when you arrive at the booking site.

Where our sample is too small to support a judgement, the app shows no score rather than a weak one.

Some screens can display clearly badged demo / sample data when real observations are unavailable. Demo content always carries a visible badge. A number next to that badge is invented and must not be relied on.

LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED: Confirm that the "deal" labelling as described is not a comparative price claim requiring substantiation under Turkish advertising rules, and that the demo-data badge is sufficient to prevent a misleading-price finding.

An alert may arrive after the fare it describes is gone.

Do not treat a notification as a reservation, a hold, or a guarantee.
